Privacy

hercomeback is general information only. Nothing you write here is shared with employers.

What we store

  • Your email address and a hashed password, so you can log in.
  • Your break journal entries, profile text, and any job descriptions you paste.
  • Pages you publish on your site.
  • The community circles you join and the notes you post there. Posts are visible to members of that circle and show your first name only.
  • Messages you send through the contact form, which go to our team's mailbox.

Where it goes

  • Your data is kept in the app's database on our server.
  • When you use AI features, the text you enter is sent to Anthropic's API to create suggestions. Anthropic's own data policy applies to that processing.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with employers.

Your control

  • Use Delete my data in the footer to remove your account, journal entries, and published pages.
  • Use Log out to end your session on this device.

Not advice

hercomeback gives general information about money, jobs, and careers. It does not give legal, tax, or medical advice.

Questions about your data? Contact us.
